Four ways to be bold & modern

These are quick style explorations to help you choose a direction. They are not final designs. Pick the one that feels most like you, or mix two (e.g. “B’s colours with D’s layout”). All names and text are placeholders; your real copy comes after discovery. Hover over the buttons and cards to see each direction’s motion style.

A Neon Night B Gradient Pop C Brutalist Bold D Editorial Tech
A

Neon Night

A dark background with an electric lime accent, terminal-style details and a soft glow. Unmistakably “developer”.

Best for: code-heavy tutorials and an instantly techy first impression.
